Finance

You know the money is moving. You find out how a week later.

Published 25 June 2026Aqueenix operations notes

What you see today

If two or three of these sound like your week, this is the department to start with.

  • Payments approved in chat, receipts in phones, the fund estimate updated when someone remembers.
  • Month-end is a hunt for documents, and the accountant waits.
  • Five entities, or five branches, each kept their own way.

What we set up

Every point below is work we have already done for a business like yours.

  • One finance rulebook: every rule, workflow and naming convention in one file that wins when documents disagree. We run five companies on it today.
  • Payments raised in a fixed format to one approval thread, approved in one word, slips filed and the group fund estimate updated the same day.
  • A daily bank update posted to the owners every evening at the same time: balance, what came in, what went out. No login, no asking.
  • Month-end on a calendar: statements and invoices by day three, reconciled by day seven, a seven-section pack to the accountant by day ten. Every transaction traced to a document.

Cash position known every evening. Approvals in minutes. A close that happens on schedule, not when someone is free.

What to watch

Four measures. If you cannot answer them today, that is the first thing we fix.

  • Days from invoice to payment
  • Days to close the month
  • Payments waiting for approval
  • Transactions without a supporting document

AI reconciles the numbers. You still decide what the business can afford.
