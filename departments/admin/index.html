Admin

You are paying for a licence to do what a shared mailbox does for free.

Published 16 July 2026Aqueenix operations notes

What you see today

If two or three of these sound like your week, this is the department to start with.

  • Letters and agreements rebuilt from the last one someone can find.
  • Files dropped anywhere, named anyhow, found by asking.
  • Tools bought on a vendor's advice, half used, renewed every year.

What we set up

Every point below is work we have already done for a business like yours.

  • We ask what the tool is for before we touch it. One client needed an extra mailbox only to hold a password; a free shared mailbox did the job, and the licence went.
  • One folder structure, one naming rule, and an inbox that files itself: drop the document in, it is named, dated and filed by entity and type.
  • Templates for letterhead, memo, quotation and agreement, so the words are drafted and the brand is carried every time, and agreements signed and filed digitally.
  • A process guide the admin team can follow and update themselves, from stamping a document to renewing a licence.

Any document in under a minute. Nothing expires unnoticed. No licence paid for a job a free tool does.

What to watch

Four measures. If you cannot answer them today, that is the first thing we fix.

  • Time to find a document
  • Renewals missed in a year
  • Licences paid for but not used
  • Documents produced from the standard template

AI files and drafts. A person decides what is worth keeping.
