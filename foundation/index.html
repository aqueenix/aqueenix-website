How a build runs

Six steps, one blueprint.

The same path for a team of five or a group of companies. The depth changes, the order does not.

  1. Map

    We walk through how work really flows today: the tools, the files, the people and the approvals.

  2. Blueprint

    One signed plan: structure, naming, roles, approvals and who owns each block.

  3. Set up

    Accounts, licences, shared spaces and access, set up on the tools you already pay for.

  4. Move and clean

    Years of files and records moved into place, labelled, and duplicates cleared.

  5. Train

    Team sessions and one-to-one coaching, new staff onboarded the same way every time.

  6. Run

    We stay after go-live, check in regularly, and improve what needs improving.

This is the outline. The detailed recipes, templates and checklists stay with our clients.
